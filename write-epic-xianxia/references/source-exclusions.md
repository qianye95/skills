# Source exclusions

Use this file only as a negative filter. Do not introduce these elements into generated work.

## Direct identifiers

Exclude source character names, factions, bloodlines, techniques, artifacts, locations, realm names, chapter titles, and catchphrases. Examples include 叶凡、九龙拉棺、荒古圣体 and other terminology uniquely associated with the source corpus.

## Motif-cluster test

A draft is derivative when several distinctive source relationships remain recognizable after nouns are renamed. Reject or redesign combinations such as:

- a fixed group of colossal sacred creatures linked to an ancient vessel or container in a cosmic setting;
- a modern-Earth departure that reproduces the source's route into a cultivation cosmos;
- a protagonist whose special body, forbidden-zone encounters, rival lineages, and advancement sequence track the source architecture;
- an ancient sovereign mystery assembled from renamed versions of the same identities, artifacts, historical gaps, and revelations;
- repeated source-like combinations of black dragons, the number nine, ancient metal containers, star travel, and witness shock.

Individual generic elements such as dragons, bells, stars, ruins, bloodlines, sacred lands, or ancient rulers are not automatically forbidden. Redesign the work when two or more appear in the same relationship or dramatic function as the source.

## Redesign procedure

When a cluster is detected, change at least three of:

- the underlying cosmological rule;
- the causal relationship;
- the character's objective;
- the material or organism involved;
- the social consequence;
- the scale and location;
- the reveal order;
- the price of using the power.

Changing names alone never resolves the problem.
