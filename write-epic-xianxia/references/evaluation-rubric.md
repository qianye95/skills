# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

## Story clarity — 25%

- 5: Objectives, causality, spatial relations, and consequences are immediately readable.
- 3: The main event is understandable but some transitions or positions are vague.
- 1: Spectacle replaces causal storytelling.

## Rhythmic control — 20%

- 5: Sentence and paragraph lengths vary with narrative function; impact lines are earned.
- 3: Some effective contrast, with noticeable repetitive patterns.
- 1: Uniform exposition or constant exclamatory fragments.

## Escalation and payoff — 20%

- 5: Each escalation changes knowledge, danger, agency, or cost and produces a payoff.
- 3: Intensity rises but some steps are adjective-only.
- 1: Repeated claims of greater power without meaningful change.

## Character agency — 15%

- 5: The viewpoint character interprets, chooses, risks, and changes the outcome.
- 3: The character acts but is often carried by spectacle.
- 1: The character merely watches explanations and reactions.

## Originality — 20%

- 5: Original setting, system, imagery, events, phrasing, and relationships; no suspicious overlap.
- 3: Original plot with derivative terminology, imagery, or scene architecture.
- 1: Renamed source material or recognizable replication.

Originality is a hard gate: do not deliver a draft below 5. A renamed cluster of two or more distinctive source motifs cannot score above 2, even if exact-text similarity is zero.
