# Corpus notes

The profile was derived from one user-supplied Chinese EPUB of the novel 《遮天》. It characterizes that work, not the author's complete bibliography.

Aggregate analysis:

- 1,821 chapter files, all parsed successfully
- 5,444,081 Han characters
- 159,761 paragraphs
- 198,053 sentences
- median sentence length: 29 Han characters
- sentence-length 10th–90th percentile: 5–47
- short sentences of 10 characters or fewer: 20.9%
- long sentences of 40 characters or more: 25.0%
- dialogue-led paragraphs: 34.2%
- dialogue-led paragraphs by chronological third: 39.9%, 34.3%, 27.5%
- median chapter-title length: 6 Han characters

These measurements support the profile's emphasis on sentence-length contrast, compact paragraphs, diminishing dialogue density as narrative scale expands, and short event-bearing chapter titles.

Do not bundle, reproduce, or distribute the source EPUB or its text. Re-run `scripts/analyze_epub.py` when analyzing a different edition or corpus.
