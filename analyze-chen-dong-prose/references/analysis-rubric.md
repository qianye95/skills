# Prose analysis rubric

## Evidence levels

- **Measured:** computed directly from the provided text.
- **Strongly evidenced:** repeated structural observation supported by multiple passages.
- **Plausible:** interpretive conclusion with credible but incomplete evidence.
- **Unsupported:** assertion based on reputation, memory, or isolated wording.

## Dimensions

### Sentence-function contrast

Measure sentence-length distribution and identify what long and short sentences do. Distinguish exposition, spatial mapping, causal chaining, recognition, reversal, and impact.

### Paragraph pulse

Examine whether paragraphs carry one dominant beat and how paragraph boundaries control attention.

### Narrative distance

Track movement between close character perception and widened historical, social, environmental, or cosmic framing.

### Progressive revelation

Map expectation, anomaly, partial explanation, contradiction, deeper reveal, and resulting question.

### Scale construction

Identify the sequence from body or object to local environment to civilizational, celestial, historical, or temporal scale. Distinguish demonstrated consequence from adjective inflation.

### Combat feedback

Map threat, decision, technique, material consequence, information update, and changed tactic. Evaluate spatial clarity and character agency.

### Status feedback

Examine how witnesses, rivals, institutions, and factions convert an event into social meaning. Repeated shock without new information scores poorly.

### Dialogue function

Classify dialogue as challenge, concealment, exposition, positioning, choice, or reaction. Measure dialogue-led paragraph density when enough text is available.

### Serialization mechanics

Inspect opening continuity, update frequency, local payoff, unresolved pressure, and the final changed question.

### Originality risk

Check source-specific names, systems, objects, relationships, event sequences, recognizable images, catchphrases, and exact textual overlap.
